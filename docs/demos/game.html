Thistledown Vale · inventory & crafting · round 1

A pantry ledger, not a shop.

The shelf is the source of truth and the bench is a workspace laid over it. Every recipe shows the shelf physically losing the ingredients before you click, so the consequence is visible first and the click only confirms it.

The one bold move
Quantities are arithmetic on the shelf.
Wheat flour 12 → 7 · 5 bakes into 5

The output count is the largest element on the screen. The button underneath it only says “Craft”.

Family — lichen & bark
groundsignal

OKLCH hue 128, analogous, calm — generated. A farming game's ground is foliage; olive is the green that reads agricultural instead of gamer-neon. Warmth lives in the bark ink and the Firelight theme, never in a cream-and-terracotta wash.

Borrowed (genre convention)

Stardew-style square inventory grid with a tab bar, a recipe list that previews ingredient shortfall on hover, and a right-hand detail panel that replaces the page instead of opening a modal.

New (this design)

The subtraction ledger, the lichen/olive family, Instrument Sans + Fraunces, the Firelight dark theme, and the state set in states. Nothing here is copied pixel-for-pixel from any game.

Anti-slop self-check

No gradient wash, no glow, no left-border accent cards (selection is a 3 px rule across the top edge), no emoji, no grey placeholder boxes, one 1.5 px icon weight throughout.

Concept, palette reason, borrowed-vs-new and the board index. The cream + terracotta + serif family was rejected: the ground is a cool sage grey and the accent is olive.
